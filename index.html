    Eat & drink

    Tables worth planning around

    Atenas eats early and simply: gallo pinto at seven, a casado at noon, a cold Imperial as the sun drops behind the Gulf of Nicoya. Hours shift; a quick look at Google Maps before you drive saves a wasted trip.

    The foodie shortlist

    Stay local: Mystique Rooftop for a tasting-menu splurge; Elevé for sunset, wine and dinner. Closer city outing: Conservatorium for a creative grill lunch in Ciudad Colón. For Sikwa, Silvestre and Grano de Oro, see the separate San José dining guide.

    Michelin status · checked 3 November 2026: Costa Rica has no Michelin-starred restaurants in the current restaurant guide. Michelin Keys recognize hotels, not restaurant stars. Sikwa’s No. 43 accolade is from Latin America’s 50 Best 2025.

    Order like you mean it

    • Casado — the lunch plate: rice, beans, plantain, salad, picadillo and your protein. ₡5,000–7,000 (≈US$11.14–15.59).
    • Gallo pinto — breakfast rice and beans with Lizano sauce, eggs and natilla.
    • Chifrijo — rice, beans, chicharrón, pico de gallo, tortilla chips. Bar food perfected.
    • Chorreada — sweet corn pancake with sour cream; the La Garita classic.
    • Café chorreado — coffee dripped through a cloth sock. Ask for it anywhere old-school.
    • Batido — fruit shake, en agua or en leche. Mora (blackberry) and cas are the local flavours.

    A destination meal

    San José, for food lovers

    Three reasons to make the drive from Colinas del Sol. Choose Sikwa for Indigenous culinary heritage and its 2025 Latin America’s 50 Best recognition; Silvestre for the full tasting-menu evening; Grano de Oro for classic courtyard elegance and à la carte choice.

    How to fit it into your trip

    Pair a confirmed lunch service with the museums, or reserve one dedicated dinner. Allow roughly 1–2 hours each way from your hotel, more in heavy traffic. For wine pairings, arrange a driver through reception. Check seating times, allergies, deposits and cancellation terms before booking. These restaurants are in San José city; Conservatorium is in Ciudad Colón and remains in the other dining section.
